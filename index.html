What we make

Every product is designed around touch — soft materials, hands-on play, and the small joy of making.

Squishy Toys

Soft, slow-rising squishies in fruit, animal and everyday shapes. Squeeze them, knead them, let the stress out.

DIY Craft Kits

Beginner-friendly kits for punch-needle embroidery and bead crafts. Everything in the box, nothing extra to buy.

Outdoor Play Toys

Light, safe outdoor toys such as our soft foam boomerang — made for parks, beaches and open fields.

About us

Tactile Reverie is a toy brand of Shantou Chenghai Fengkai Trading Co., Ltd., based in Chenghai, Shantou — a city known for toy manufacturing. We design toys that are soft to the touch and simple to enjoy, and we sell them through TikTok Shop in Thailand and other markets.

Our focus is on handmade-feeling products: things you squeeze, poke, stitch and finish yourself. No screens needed — just hands, patience and a little imagination.
